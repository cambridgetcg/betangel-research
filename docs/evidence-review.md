# Does anyone actually make money with Bet Angel?

Reviewed **2026-10-09**. This is a bounded review of public first-person records, vendor documentation and one primary research paper; it is not a systematic census of customers.

## What the evidence supports

There are identifiable people reporting profits while using Bet Angel, including a historical year-long diary. We did **not** find independently audited records sufficient to estimate a typical customer's net ROI, probability of success or time to profitability. Absence of an audit in this search does not establish that every claim is false.

## Independent research is a different evidence category

[Gonçalves et al., *Convolutional Attention in Betting Exchange Markets*, 14 Oct 2025, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.16008v1) studies UK pre-race price prediction using a custom framework, not a Bet Angel customer account. Figure 22 and Table 9 describe **simulated** results over 30 held-out days and 134 trading attempts in one category. The paper acknowledges queue and market-impact limitations. We did not locate an explicit commission deduction in the displayed full text; after-fee profitability is therefore unverified here. This is a research lead, not evidence of earned cash or a reproducible Bet Angel ROI.

## Why neither success nor failure threads give a success rate

The sample is self-selected. Successful traders may advertise or sell tools; discouraged users may post disproportionately in help threads; people who quietly stop disappear. Old market conditions may not persist. Screenshots may omit losses, deposits, other accounts, costs, conditional liabilities or the denominator behind a claimed return.

For a stronger individual record, seek a continuous, specified period of settled account P&L reconciled with deposits/withdrawals, commissions and other fees; label practice/live status; disclose capital, drawdown and hours worked. Independent reconciliation would improve confidence, but one successful trader would still not establish typical-user returns.

**Working judgement:** investigate a mechanism and test it. Do not budget an income stream from these anecdotes, a ready-made automation file or the fact that a platform is capable of running continuously.
