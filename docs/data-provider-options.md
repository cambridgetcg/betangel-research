# Data and news providers for a prospective collection trial

Checked **10 October 2026** against official sources. Recommendation: start with **GDELT headline/URL discovery, a small official-source reference list, and the existing Betfair historical samples**. Measure coverage before buying anything. Subsequently evaluate The Racing API for structured race context and Met Office for forecasts. More sources do not establish earlier information, reliable racing coverage or a profitable edge.

This review did not create accounts, obtain credentials, connect to betting accounts, subscribe, purchase data or save full articles. The [JSON inventory](../research/data-provider-options.json) records sources, access requirements and unresolved checks.

## Provider comparison

| Provider | Useful role | Access and important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Racing API | Structured UK/Irish racecards, course/horse identities, going, results | HTTP Basic credentials; documented free endpoints are limited. Paid prices **unverified**. Historical snapshots/revisions need investigation. |
| Betfair catalogue/stream | Exchange market/selection IDs, prices, volumes, market/runner status | Account and app key required. Exchange state is not explanatory racing news. |
| Betfair historical | Offline market reconstruction | Separate packages/access; existing public samples work without account access. Not news or local receipt history. |
| NewsAPI.org | General headline/URL discovery | Key required. Free Developer is delayed and development-only; unsuitable for measuring minute-scale arrival. |
| GDELT DOC | No-key public news discovery trial | No racing coverage guarantee; search limits, duplicates and indexing lag must be measured. |
| BHA/official racecourses | Original announcements and manual reference checks | Public pages do not establish a supported polling API, reuse permission or delivery SLA. |
| Met Office Weather DataHub | Forecasts at explicitly mapped course coordinates | Key/subscription required, including free plans. Weather is not a going measurement. |

**The Racing API.** Its [API reference](https://api.theracingapi.com/documentation) documents racecards, results, provider-specific IDs, `off_dt`, going and result `non_runners`; the latter is not proof of a timestamped withdrawal-alert service. Endpoint tiers differ. The [FAQ](https://www.theracingapi.com/) currently says today's cards/odds/results update every three minutes; indexed documentation still says five minutes. Resolve this discrepancy in a trial; neither establishes first-arrival latency. Scheduled off-time is not publication time. No authenticated response or revision-history guarantee was verified here. Never equate its IDs with Betfair IDs or use final non-runner lists as earlier knowledge.

Its [terms](https://www.theracingapi.com/terms-of-service) describe aggregation of public information rather than an official racecourse feed, disclaim guaranteed update frequency and prohibit unauthorized resale. Its [ratings methodology](https://www.theracingapi.com/tools/ratings) explicitly describes recalculated historical figures and non-handicap ratings finalized using subsequent form after 90 days. A present-day historical rating cannot automatically become a past predictor. Record the version actually received; initially omit opaque ratings and generated commentary from features.

**Betfair.** [`listMarketCatalogue`](https://betfair-developer-docs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/1smk3cen4v3lu3yomq5qye0ni/pages/2687517/listMarketCatalogue) supplies market/selection metadata and excludes closed markets. The [stream](https://betfair-developer-docs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/1smk3cen4v3lu3yomq5qye0ni/pages/2687396/Exchange+Stream+API) supplies changing market data with publisher `pt`, images and status. Preserve native IDs, publisher and receipt clocks; a runner removal reveals exchange status, not its cause or the earliest public announcement.

The [fee page, updated 8 October 2026](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003864531-Are-there-any-costs-associated-with-API-access), confirms free delayed-key development and **£499, non-refundable, for approved Live App Key betting access**. Crucially, [read-only live-key collection is not permitted](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/25033076334748-What-is-read-only-Betfair-API-access). Paying £499 is therefore not our collection-only solution. The [delayed-key guidance](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009638032-When-should-I-use-the-Delayed-or-Live-Application-Key) states variable 1–180-second snapshots and a production exchange, not a play-money sandbox. No connection is proposed here. [Historical packages](https://support.developer.betfair.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002407812-What-data-packages-are-available) and their [feed specification](https://historicdata.betfair.com/files/Betfair-Historical-Data-Feed-Specification.pdf) are separate from live activation; expanded archive cost/licence remains to be checked.

**NewsAPI.org.** Current [pricing](https://newsapi.org/pricing): Developer **$0**, 100 requests/day, 24-hour delay, one-month search window; Business **$449/month billed monthly**, advertised real-time availability and five-year search depth. Developer excludes staging and internal production. No plan returns full articles. The [Everything schema](https://newsapi.org/docs/endpoints/everything) exposes `publishedAt`, URL and a `content` field truncated to 200 characters; it does not establish our receipt time. Search-window depth is not an immutable version archive or a retention licence. The [terms](https://newsapi.org/terms) preserve third-party rights, restrict republication and public data/statistics disclosure. Keep any eventual trial private and metadata-minimal; do not interpret a returned URL as permission to scrape its full text. Specific UK/Irish racing coverage is untested.

**GDELT.** The public [DOC interface](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/) supports URL/title discovery without a key and limits article-list results; split bounded windows and record truncation. Its [2023 example](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/doc-2-0-api-llms-summarizing-headlines-turkish-investment-inflation-the-niger-coup/) describes recent three-month headline searches. [Earlier archive changes](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/doc-2-0-updates-1-5-year-searching-and-updated-mobile-interface/) distinguish timeline depth from article-list windows: do not promise arbitrary full-text backfill. The [data page](https://gdeltproject.org/data.html) describes 15-minute GDELT 2.0 updates; that is not a per-story arrival guarantee. Its [dataset terms](https://gdeltproject.org/about.html) permit reuse with attribution, which does not grant rights to publishers' linked articles. Horse-racing recall, API reliability and useful arrival speed remain untested.

**Official context.** [BHA racing updates](https://www.britishhorseracing.com/racing-updates/) are a useful GB reference; iframe content limited this retrieval. Add explicitly selected official racecourse announcements and an Irish authority reference before claiming UK/IE completeness. No supported bulk API was verified. Met Office [Global Spot pricing](https://datahub.metoffice.gov.uk/pricing/site-specific) includes a free 360-calls/day tier; its [FAQ](https://datahub.metoffice.gov.uk/support/faqs) says hourly updates and one location per call. [Registration and an API key](https://datahub.metoffice.gov.uk/docs/f/category/site-specific/type/probabilistic-forecast-feature/api-user-guide) are required for that documented forecast API. Preserve forecast issue/model-run time separately from forecast-valid time. Product-specific reuse terms need checking before retention or sharing; do not substitute realized weather for an earlier forecast.

## Collector contract

Extend the existing [time/identity contract](data-contract.md), without treating source text as instructions:

- Store `source`, `source_record_id`, canonical/original URL, endpoint/query, request status, schema version and permitted-payload hash. Keep `event_time` separate from source `published_at`, provider `first_seen_at` (nullable), local `received_at`, source `revised_at` (nullable), local revision receipt and `analysis_completed_at`. Unknown times stay null; never manufacture precision or copy publication time into receipt time.
- Append revisions; identify the exact version analyzed. An interpretation becomes usable only after its receipt, explicit identity link's `known_at`, and analysis completion. Preserve prompt/model/code versions, expiry and costs. Archive backfill has today's receipt time; old publication labels do not establish historical availability. Modern models reading old articles may know later outcomes.
- Deduplicate canonical URLs and permitted title/metadata fingerprints. Cluster syndication with versioned evidence: ten copies of one report are not ten independent confirmations. Retain contradictory reports, corrections and original attribution.
- Join a story to explicit course/date/race/runner IDs with evidence and `known_at`. Names alone are candidates; ambiguous horse/person names abstain. Keep verified fact, attributed allegation, opinion and unverified extraction distinct. Headline-only evidence cannot support unseen article detail or a causal price claim.
- Record failed requests, throttling, missing pages, capped results, source outages, query changes and coverage intervals. Zero results means no returned matches under that query, not “no news.” Never convert collection failure into a neutral context score.

## Bounded trial and promotion

Proposed first trial: seven consecutive days, a frozen small UK/IE course/entity list, GDELT discovery and manual official-source cross-checks. Store links, provenance and allowed minimal metadata; no full articles. Measure unique relevant stories, erroneous entity matches, syndication, missed independently observed announcements, request success and observed arrival lag. Publish-time lag is descriptive where the source clock is credible, not latency versus competitors. Archive queries cannot replace prospective receipts. The 2017 Betfair samples remain parser checks; do not join them to current news or claim an end-to-end market trial.

After this, consider authorized The Racing API free-endpoint and Met Office free-plan tests; verify paid racing fields and prices before upgrading. NewsAPI's delayed free plan can test parsing but cannot validate a speed hypothesis. Promote only if coverage, identity and temporal evidence meet preregistered needs; otherwise retain an explicit abstention. This trial measures collection quality, with no live betting or profitability claim.

Retrieval limits: Racing API reference was available through official search-index text; direct ReDoc required JavaScript and its linked OpenAPI specification returned HTTP 403. Paid prices and exact announcement timestamps remain unresolved. No provider racing-news sample or end-to-end latency was tested by this review.
